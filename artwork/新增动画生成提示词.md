Preserve the reference's large OPEN ROUND luminous violet-to-cyan eyes with white STAR highlights in all open-eye poses. No sleepy half-lidded dead stare except the explicitly sleeping poses. No redesign or simplification.
Create ONE high-resolution portrait spritesheet, TWO columns and THREE rows, EXACTLY SIX separated complete characters, generous empty transparent gutters so sprites do not touch. True RGBA transparent background, no floor shadows, no text, no symbols, no frames, no outside human hands. Face playfully surprised but happy with fully open round bright starry eyes, NOT sad, NOT scared, no tears. Keep head upright at same position for animation.

Right repeats same sleeping pose with a subtle breathing expansion and slightly relaxed hands. No bed, no pillow, no floating Z letters.
